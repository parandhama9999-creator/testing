## 1. Overview
OrderFlow Crypto Pro ("the App") is an educational market-visualisation and **simulated** paper-trading tool. It is not financial advice. The App does not place real trades and does not hold, transfer or handle real funds or crypto assets. You do not need an account to use it.

## 2. Data the App stores on your device
These items are stored only in your device's local app storage and are never sent to us:
- Chart and display settings (symbol, timeframe, colours, alert preferences).
- Simulated paper-trading data (virtual balance, positions, trade history).
- A cached copy of your Pro entitlement status and ad-frequency counters.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes this information.

## 3. Market data
The App downloads public market data (prices, trades, order books) directly from public endpoints of Binance (fapi.binance.com, fstream.binance.com) and Bybit (api.bybit.com, stream.bybit.com). These requests carry no personal information beyond what any internet connection exposes (for example your IP address) and are subject to those exchanges' own privacy policies.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, coarse location derived from IP, and ad-interaction data to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads load. You can change your choice at any time from the consent form, and you can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android settings.
More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)
Purchases of OrderFlow Pro are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. Google provides us with purchase tokens and status, which the App uses only to unlock Pro features.
