## 2. Data the App stores on your device
These items are stored only in your device's local app storage and are never sent to us:

- Simulated paper-trading data (virtual balance, positions, trade history).

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes this information.

## 3. Market data
The App downloads public market data (prices, trades, order books) directly from public endpoints of Binance (fapi.binance.com, fstream.binance.com) and Bybit (api.bybit.com, stream.bybit.com). These requests carry no personal information beyond what any internet connection exposes (for example your IP address) and are subject to those exchanges' own privacy policies.
