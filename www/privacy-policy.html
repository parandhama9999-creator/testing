Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2025 Last updated: 27 October 2025 Version 1.0

Quick summary — plain English

  • We do not collect, store, or share your personal data on our own servers.
  • All your settings and preferences are saved only on your device.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing — we never see your payment details.
  • Market data is fetched read-only from Binance and Bybit — we do not send your data to them.
  • This app is for analysis only — it is not financial advice and does not execute trades.

1 Who We Are

OrderFlow Crypto Pro ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is operated by Parandama. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at parandhama9999@gmail.com.

2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the OrderFlow Crypto Pro Android application (package name com.parandama.orderflow) and any related services. By installing and using the App you agree to the practices described here.

3 Information We Collect

We do not operate a backend server and therefore do not collect or receive personal information ourselves. Data is handled as follows:

Stored only on your device (local storage)

  • App settings (chart preferences, colour themes, indicator settings)
  • Saved chart templates you create
  • Risk disclaimer acceptance status
  • Paper-trading account state
  • Whale-alert and price-alert preferences

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear the App's data from Android settings.

Market data requests

The App sends requests to Binance and Bybit public APIs to retrieve live and historical price data. These requests contain only standard HTTP headers (including your IP address and device's user-agent string as set by your operating system). We do not send any personally identifying information in these requests, and we do not store the results on any server.

4 Third-Party Services

The following third parties are integrated into the App and may collect data under their own privacy policies:

Service Purpose Data collected by them Privacy policy
Google AdMob Displaying advertisements Advertising ID, device identifiers, interaction data, approximate location (country/region), network type Google Privacy Policy
Google Play Billing Processing in-app subscriptions and purchases Purchase history, payment method (handled entirely by Google — we receive only a purchase token) Google Privacy Policy
Binance Futures API Real-time and historical price/trade data IP address (via standard HTTPS request), no account data Binance Privacy Policy
Bybit API Fallback real-time and historical price/trade data IP address (via standard HTTPS request), no account data Bybit Privacy Policy

For EU/EEA users, Google AdMob displays a UMP consent form on first launch so you can choose whether to allow personalised advertising. Your choice is respected and stored. You can change this preference at any time through the App's settings.

5 Advertising and the Advertising ID

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads to non-subscribed users. AdMob may use your device's Android Advertising ID (AAID) to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. You can:

  • Reset or opt out of interest-based advertising at any time via Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Purchase a Pro subscription to remove all ads entirely, in which case AdMob is not loaded.

6 In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The App offers optional subscriptions (Pro Monthly, Pro Yearly) and a one-time Pro Lifetime purchase via Google Play Billing. All payment processing is handled exclusively by Google. We receive only a non-reversible purchase token confirming the transaction. We never see, process, or store your payment card number or bank details.

7 Data We Do Not Collect

We explicitly do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account credentials
  • Your precise location
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Trading account credentials for any exchange
  • Any data from other apps on your device
  • Crash reports or analytics (there is no analytics SDK in the App)

8 How We Use Information

Because we collect no personal data ourselves, our use is limited to:

  • Verifying your purchase status so Pro features unlock correctly (via the Google Play purchase token)
  • Displaying advertisements through AdMob to non-Pro users

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with any party for any purpose beyond what is described in this policy.

9 Data Retention and Deletion

All local device data is retained until you uninstall the App or manually clear its data (Android Settings → Apps → OrderFlow Crypto Pro → Clear data). We have no server-side data to delete on your behalf. If you want to delete data held by Google AdMob or Google Play, see Google's own data deletion tools at myaccount.google.com.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access data held about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing

  • Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at parandhama9999@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. For data held by Google, please use Google's own account tools.

11 Children's Privacy

This App is intended for users aged 18 and over. Cryptocurrency derivatives trading involves substantial financial risk that is not appropriate for minors. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or 16 in the EU/EEA). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us immediately at parandhama9999@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete such information.

12 Security

All communication between the App and third-party APIs (Binance, Bybit, Google) is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, the risk of a data breach on our end is minimal. Device-local data is protected by Android's standard app sandboxing.

13 International Transfers

By using the App, your device may communicate with servers operated by Google (United States) and Binance/Bybit (various jurisdictions). These transfers are covered by each party's own data transfer mechanisms. For EU users, Google's services are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses and/or adequacy decisions where applicable.
